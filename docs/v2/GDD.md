# GDD v2: the pact game (working title: Brimble's Bargain)

Status: design for build. All numbers here match `docs/v2/data/*.json`. If they differ, the JSON wins. Data is generated by `scripts/gen-v2-data.py`.

Balance status: only the base machine (no charms) was simulated. Everything past debt 2 is designed by ratio and is **not verified**. See section 17.

## 1. One-line pitch

You signed a pact with a small-time devil. Pay his rising debts with a rickety fortune machine, build a charm engine, push the Heat, sign worse deals, and find out whose debt it really is.

Title options: **Brimble's Bargain**, **Pact & Wick**, **Moth Fair Pact**.

## 2. Premise and people

**Place.** A small night fair that sets up on waste ground and is gone by morning. Striped tents, paper lanterns, moths.

**Nim** (player character, 19). Her family's lantern stall burned down on the first night of the fair. Her brother Tao lit the match. To rebuild it she signed a paper she did not read. Voice: dry, practical, counts everything. Sample lines:
- "Sixty coins. I've paid more for worse."
- "You wrote this clause in a smaller pen."
- "I am not lucky. I am organised."

**Mr. Brimble** (the devil). Short, pot-bellied, moth-eaten top hat, patched tailcoat. Runs one fortune machine that he repairs with string. Menacing only because the paperwork is real. Voice: bored clerk, dry humour, never shouts. Sample lines:
- "Three nights. Sixty coins. Or I take the candle. Company policy."
- "That was luck. Luck is not in the contract."
- "A deal. Good for you now. Good for me later. That is what a deal is."
- "You're pushing the Heat. I admire that in a customer I am about to lose."
- (after a Scorch) "The machine is fine. Your purse is lighter. Everyone learns."
- (when you refuse) "Clean hands. How dull."

**The collateral.** Nim's candle: her life flame, kept on the shelf on top of the machine. Missing a deadline means Brimble lifts the snuffer (`devil/collect`). The run ends. Nim wakes at the hideout: Brimble cannot afford to lose a paying customer, so he relights it. He says so.

**The twist.** The debt is not Nim's. The fine print names Brimble as the debtor: he owes Head Office for the machine, and every customer pact is a co-signature on his loan. The fire started in his machine's wiring, not from Tao's match. The player learns this in pieces: ending 1, the Armchair hideout lines, contract 7 ("Head Office Audit").

**Three endings.**
1. **Paid in Full** (clear contract 3). Nim's page is stamped. The stall is rebuilt. Brimble is still there the next night with a new customer. Pip slips Nim the real contract.
2. **Bought the Machine** (clear contract 7 without signing a single deal in that run). Nim pays Head Office directly and owns Brimble's debt paper. He is free and has nowhere to go. He moves into the armchair.
3. **New Management** (clear contract 10 while carrying 5 or more curses). Head Office promotes the best earner. Nim gets the hat. Brimble becomes her clerk. The last line is his: "Three nights. Sixty coins. You say it, I've retired."

**Hideout.** An abandoned ghost-train carriage (`bg/hideout`). It starts with a cot. Ten pieces are bought with Wax between runs (section 13). Pieces open options, not flat power.

## 3. The goal on screen at all times

Top bar of the run screen, never hidden:

`DEBT 3 of 8   1,240 / 2,600   Night 2 of 3   Spins 4`

- A candle on the machine burns down as nights pass (the deadline, readable without text).
- Under it: Purse (unbanked coins), Wick, Heat meter (0 to 10), Luck, the Favourite flag.
- The BANK button shows how much is still owed.

## 4. The machine

5 columns x 3 rows, 15 cells, row-major index 0 to 14. Every cell rolls on its own from the weight table.

### Symbols (`symbols.json`)

| id | tier | value | weight | group |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| moth | 1 | 2 | 14 | low |
| candle | 1 | 2 | 14 | low |
| tooth | 2 | 3 | 11 | low |
| key | 2 | 3 | 11 | low |
| eye | 3 | 4 | 9 | mid |
| mask | 3 | 5 | 8 | mid |
| crow | 4 | 6 | 7 | high |
| lantern | 4 | 8 | 5 | high |
| crown | 5 | 12 | 3 | high |
| wisp (wild) | - | - | 2 | - |
| sigil (pact sigil) | - | - | 2 | - |
| ash (curse symbol) | - | - | 0 + Heat | - |

- **Wisp**: wild. Takes the value of the pattern's symbol. A pattern needs at least one real symbol.
- **Sigil**: never matches. Each one that lands gives +1 Wick. Three or more in one spin: one free random trick.
- **Ash**: our own rule. Its weight is `2.0 x Heat` plus curse additions, so it only exists because the player pushed Heat or signed for it. It blocks patterns. Three or more Ash in one spin is a **Scorch**: the spin pays nothing, 10% of the purse burns (rounded up), Heat drops by 3.

### The Favourite and Luck

Before every spin the machine picks one scoring symbol by weight and shows it on a flag: the **Favourite**. Its weight for that spin is multiplied by `1 + Luck`.

- Base Luck 4, cap 12. Nim has +1.
- Pity: a spin with no pattern gives +1 Luck until the next pattern pays.
- The player sees the Favourite before deciding to Stoke or to use a trick. This is the main pre-spin decision.

Base machine, no charms, 30,000 simulated spins each (Heat 0): Luck 3 pays 5.4 coins a spin with 38% empty spins. Luck 4 pays 7.8 with 31%. Luck 6 pays 13.7 with 19%.

### Score, in the order shown on screen

Per pattern: `VALUE x SYMBOL MULT x PATTERN MULT`, rounded down. Three numbers light up left to right, then the product.

Spin: `SUM OF PATTERNS x HEAT x BONUS`, rounded down, then flat coin gains are added.

After each spin the three biggest contributors (charm, trick, Heat) show as chips. Tap the total for the full list.

## 5. Heat (the risk meter)

- STOKE button: +1 Heat per tap, before a spin, free, up to 10. The player cannot lower it by hand.
- Each Heat level: all payouts +20%. Ash weight +2.0 (critic pass: Heat 4 is no longer free; the Luck 3 numbers below predate it).
- Heat cools by 2 at the start of each round. A Scorch drops it by 3.
- Measured on the base machine at Luck 3: Heat 0 pays 5.4, Heat 4 pays 7.4 with 2% Scorch, Heat 6 pays 7.9 with 5%, Heat 10 pays 8.8 with 16%. Purse burn is on top of that.

Heat is the push-your-luck layer and it is always the player's hand on the button, never a random disaster.

## 6. Run structure

A **contract** is a list of debts. Each debt has **3 rounds** ("nights"). Each round:

1. **Terms.** Three terms cards are drawn from 12. Pick one. It sets the spins and a rule for the round.
2. **Spins.** Stoke or not, use tricks or not, spin. After the reels stop, post-spin tricks may be used, then COLLECT. With no post-spin trick in the pocket, collect is automatic.
3. **Bank.** At any time between spins the player may bank purse coins into the debt.
4. **Round end.** Interest is paid on the purse. Then the pedlar wagon (shop) opens.

**Deadline.** At the end of the last round the purse is banked automatically. If the debt is not covered, in this order: a Spare Candle trick burns, or the Co-signer charm burns, or the Snooze deal is used, or Brimble offers **the Pawn** once per run (burn one charm, take one random curse, get one more round). Otherwise he collects and the run ends.

**Debt paid.** Stamp. Then Brimble offers two deals (section 10). Purse and Heat carry over.

**Early pay.** Paying a debt with rounds left skips them: +2 Wick per unused round and one random trick. The cost is the spins and interest you did not take.

**Interest.** At round end: 5% of the purse, capped at 25% of the current debt. Coins in the purse earn; coins in the debt are safe from a Scorch. That is the keep-or-bank choice.

## 7. Charms (`charms.json`, 107)

- Passive items in **5 slots** (max 8: shop slot for 10 Wick, deals, Pip). Slot order is left to right and is the evaluation order. The player can reorder.
- Rarity: common 36, uncommon 38, rare 20, legendary 10.
- 74 are in the pool from the first run. 30 are unlocked by achievements. To avoid pool dilution, shop rolls weight a family x2.5 if you already hold a charm of it.
- Sell for 50% of the price at any time in the shop.
- Every charm is data: `rules: [{trigger, cond, effects}]`. See `EFFECTS.md`.

Ten synergy families (tag = family, some charms carry a second tag):

| family | what it does | count |
|---|---|---|
| swarm | low symbols, head-count on the grid | 11 |
| ember | Heat, Stoke, pay at high Heat | 11 |
| hex | curses and Ash become income | 11 |
| ledger | purse, interest, banking, prices | 10 |
| pact | deals, Sigils, Wick | 11 |
| omen | Luck and the Favourite | 11 |
| stitch | shapes and positions | 10 |
| wisp | wilds and transforms | 10 |
| tinker | tricks and the bench | 10 |
| crown | high symbols, big lines | 9 |

Cross-family builds the data is meant to allow:
- **Burning ledger**: ember + hex + ledger. Sit at Heat 8, eat Scorches for Wick and mult (Ash Cake, Martyr's Splinter), bank early.
- **Moth flood**: swarm + omen + wisp. Changeling turns Moths into the Favourite, Dust Queen retriggers, Night Swarm scales with head-count.
- **Paper court**: crown + tinker + stitch. Pull low symbols out at the bench, Scrap Heap pays for each removal, Paper King and Long Hem on big lines.
- **Signed everything**: pact + hex. Firm Handshake and Cracked Mirror scale with deals and curses; Black Cat turns curses into Luck.

## 9. Tricks (`tricks.json`, 20)

One-use actions in **3 pockets**. Bought in the shop, found from Sigils and early pay. Each has a timing: `pre` (before a spin), `post` (after the reels stop, before collect), `any`, `shop`, `auto`.

Nudge, Thumbtack (hold a column), Do-over (respin a row), Double Stamp, Switcheroo, Green Brush, Straw Broom, Hourglass, Ice Cube, Chili, Sleeve Pocket, Stencil, Wish Coin, Tool Roll, Spiral Token, Party Horn, Hand Mirror, Thumbprint Note, Salt, Spare Candle.

The spin result is the start of a decision, not the end of it.

## 10. Devil deals (`deals.json`, 24) and curses (`curses.json`, 16)

After every paid debt Brimble offers **2 deals** (3 with Fine Print or Pip). Each is a strong boon plus a named permanent curse, both shown in full before signing. SIGN one or REFUSE both. Refusing gives +3 Wick.

- Curses stack, last for the run, and change rules (no interest, dead centre cell, hidden Favourite, a charm slot lost).
- Three deals give a random curse. One (The Black Envelope) gives a legendary charm for two random curses.
- Curses are also a resource: hex charms, Doc Rattle and ending 3 want them.
- Brimble's lines react to counts: deals signed, deals refused, current Heat, curses held (4 line sets, 6 lines each, to be written in `i18n`).

## 11. Shop (the pedlar wagon, run by Pip)

Opens after every round.

- 4 charm offers, 2 trick offers, 2 bench jobs.
- Charm prices: common 4 coins, uncommon 7 coins, rare 5 Wick, legendary 9 Wick. Coin prices x S.
- Rarity odds by debt: debts 1 to 2: 62 / 30 / 8 / 0. Debts 3 to 5: 50 / 32 / 15 / 3. Debt 6 and later: 38 / 34 / 21 / 7.
- Reroll: 2 coins x S, +1 x S for each reroll in the same visit.
- Lock: one offer can be locked for free and stays for the next visit.
- Extra charm slot: 10 Wick, once per run.
- **Wick** is the second currency: rare and legendary charms, Gild, Pull Out, Lift a Curse, the slot, four tricks. Sources: start 3, Sigils, Wick Run, early pay, refusing deals, charms.

## 13. Meta progression

- **Wax**: 3 per debt paid, plus the contract reward, plus achievements, plus 10 for a daily run. Never bought. Spent on the hideout.
- **Hideout** (`hideout.json`, 10): Cot 0, Stove 25, Shelf 40, Lanterns 60, Workbench 80, Desk 100, Keepsake Box 120, Moth House 150, Mirror 200, Armchair 300. Total 1,075 Wax.
- **Achievements** (`achievements.json`, 43): 30 unlock a charm, 5 unlock a character, 8 give Wax.
- **Contract ladder**: 10 contracts.
- **Daily run**: seed from the UTC date. Fixed character, contract 2, one forced terms card on round 1 and one starting curse, both from the seed. First attempt of the day is the scored one. Score = debts paid, then total coins. Stored locally (no server in v2).
- **Collection** screen: charms seen, curses carried, endings.

## 16. Money and store safety

- Virtual coins only, no cash-out, nothing converts back to money.
- Spins, Wick, tricks, charms and Wax are never sold. No paid random items.
- Ads: interstitial only after a run ends, at most one per 180 seconds, never in the first run. Rewarded, max 6 a day: one free shop reroll, or double Wax at run end.
- IAP: remove ads; cosmetics (machine skins, Brimble hats, hideout wallpaper); starter pack (remove ads plus one skin plus 100 Wax, the only Wax ever sold and it is a fixed amount).
- No in-app age gate. Delete `src/ui/agegate.js`.
- Wording: never "bet", "wager", "stake", "casino", "slot", "jackpot", "gamble" in the game or the store text. The player never chooses an amount to risk; a spin costs nothing. Screen words: machine, spin, terms, pattern, Full Fair. Still answer the store questionnaires honestly and expect 12+ or 13+ and blocks in some countries. Thai rules were not checked.

## 17. What is not verified

- Only the base machine was simulated. Debt 3 and later, charm strength, deal strength and all ten contracts need a bot sim (`scripts/sim-v2.mjs`, under 2 minutes, see `TECH.md`). Target for a greedy bot with Nim: contract 1 clear rate 60 to 75%.
- Run length in minutes is an estimate.
- Thai and Turkish texts are composed from parts by the generator. They need a native read (`STRINGS-REVIEW.md`).

## 19. From `GPT-IDEAS.md`: used, changed, rejected

Used: tricks that act on the result (Peg Crank became Nudge, Safety Pin became Thumbtack, Forgery Stamp became Stencil, Sleeve Snatch became Sleeve Pocket, Reverse Ticket became Do-over); a visible, player-driven risk meter (its Heat became our Heat, with our own numbers); the Hollow Halo pattern; the Crooked Road shape (our Vee); Tomorrow's Problem (our Thumbprint Note and the Advance deal); Sealed Jam Jar (Clay Jar charm); Exact Change (charm and achievement); the announced marquee (our Favourite flag); showing the top three contributors after each spin; a hideout that unlocks options, not flat stats; a devil whose lines react to behaviour; no menu before the first spin.

Fixed: it proposed 3 contracts of 4 spins (12 spins a run). That is the shallowness the owner rejected, so runs are 5 to 9 debts. It proposed claiming only one pattern per spin; that removes the multi-pattern explosion the genre is loved for, so all patterns pay. Its Barker's Bet is a literal bet and a store risk; dropped. Its numbers for the reference game (7% interest, symbol values, deadline 7 and 8 amounts) conflict with our research and are not used. Its 6 symbols are too few for weight play; we use 9 plus 3 specials.

Not used in v2: Married Corners and Toll Bridge (two-symbol patterns need a second matcher), Variety Applause, fair weather and visitors.

## Critic pass (v2.1)

- **Boss debts.** Debt 3 and the last debt of every contract carry a named rule, shown in red under the goal line: The Auditor (3-cell patterns pay half), The Collector (Ash +3 weight), The Veiled Clerk (Favourite hidden), The Miser (no interest), The Timekeeper (-1 spin per terms card), The Head Inspector (3-cell patterns pay nothing). Data: `config.json` `bosses`, `contracts.json` `bosses`.
- **Favourite redraw.** Before a spin, tap the Favourite chip: the first redraw each round is free, then 1 Wick each. Cloudy Ball adds one free redraw.
- **Slot order.** Understudy (copies the charm to its right), Opening Act (x1.5 in the leftmost slot), The Closer (x1.5 in the rightmost filled slot).
- **Economy.** Flat coin effects scale by S^0.75 (prices still use S). All symbols gain +1 value from debt 4 and again from debt 7. Interest 8%. Contract 1 debts are 60/150/400/650/1300 (on-ramp).
