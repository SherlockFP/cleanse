Privacy policy: Small Luck Pier

Last updated: 2026-11-16. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL_TODO

In short

There is no account, no chat and no server of ours. Your progress is saved on your phone only. Data leaves the phone only through the four services listed below: ads, purchases, usage statistics and crash reports. The game is for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

What stays on your phone

Your save (runs, boathouse, album, settings, language, ad and purchase counters) is stored on the device. Nothing in it is sent to us. "Export save" gives you a text code you can keep yourself. "Delete my data" in Settings clears the save from the device. Deleting the app removes it too.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files, messages, health data or precise location. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before the first ad request. You can change your answer later in Settings under "Ad choices".
  • iPhone (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, ads still work but are not personalized. The game plays the same either way.
  • Ads and purchases. Reward ads are optional. Buying "Remove ads" ends the ads between runs. Purchases can be restored from Settings.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset it or switch personalization off in your phone's system settings.
  • Deleting ad and purchase data. We hold none ourselves. Use each service's own controls (links above) or write to us and we will pass the request on.

Game rules note

The first tide of your first run is a fixed tutorial. Every spin after it is drawn from the bag. Coins and Planks exist only inside the game. They cannot be cashed out, traded or won as prizes, and no turn, peg, coin or odds is ever sold or given for an ad.

Children

Small Luck Pier is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has used the game, write to us.

Your rights by region

EEA, UK (GDPR and UK GDPR)

Ads and analytics rest on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting the game. Purchases and crash reports are processed to run the game and keep it stable. You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and object to processing, and complain to your data protection authority. RevenueCat and Google process data for us or as their own controllers under their policies, and data can be transferred outside your country under the safeguards those companies describe (such as standard contractual clauses).

California (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell personal information. Ad partners may receive advertising identifiers and ad interaction data; you can opt out of this sharing through "Ad choices" in Settings or your phone's advertising settings.

Turkey (KVKK)

The data controller is the developer named by the contact address above. Data is processed on your explicit consent for ads and analytics, and for running the game for the rest. It is transferred abroad to the services listed above. You can use the rights in KVKK article 11 by writing to the contact address.
