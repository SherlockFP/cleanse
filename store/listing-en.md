## Long description (4000 max)

Deni owns a wrecked pier, a boathouse with no roof and her grandfather's hand-cranked paper reel. The harbor co-op wants its money. Kaptan Orhan is counting.

TURN, PEG, TURN AGAIN
Turn the crank. Like two lanterns? Clip a clothes peg on them and they stay for the next turn. Three in a line fills the lantern string. Fill it before the tide goes out.

THREE-MINUTE RUNS
Five tides, six turns each. Play it with one thumb while the tea brews.

BUILD YOUR LUCK
Pick up 35 trinkets that bend the rules. Add and remove symbols from the bag. Every percentage is on screen.

A HARBOR THAT GROWS
Collect planks. Patch the roof, light the stove, hang the lamp. The boathouse slowly becomes a teahouse.

EVERY DAY, A NEW TIDE
One Daily Tide for the whole harbor. Six installments of story, and a drawer Orhan will not talk about.

PRINTED BY HAND
Two-ink riso colors, lino-cut lines, paper you can almost feel.

Fair play: the game uses virtual coins only. There is no real-money play and there are no prizes. No turn, peg, coin or odds is sold or given for an ad. Purchases are ad removal and one starter pack with a fixed number of planks; an optional ad can double the planks of a run. For adults.
