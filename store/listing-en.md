## Long description (4000 max)

Nim is 19 and broke. Her family's food cart burned down on the first night of a new soi market. To rebuild it she signed a paper she did not read. The other name on it is Mr. Ruby: a cheeky red devil in sunglasses and a floral shirt who runs a neon fortune machine at the edge of the night market.

SPIN, SPICE UP, PAY THE DEBT
Spin the 5 by 3 machine. Match three in a row or a column. Spice Up for more pay, but too much Soot sets off a Flare-up and burns your purse. Pay each debt before the nights run out.

CLIMB THROUGH THAILAND
Ten places, one step up each time: a soi cart, the train market, Yaowarat, Khao San Road, a beach town in low season, Chiang Mai in the haze, a Phuket beach club, a riverside boutique, a Sukhumvit condo launch and, at the top, the sky bar. Every place changes the rules.

MAKE A DEAL WITH THE DEVIL
After each debt Mr. Ruby slides over a paper: a boon now and a curse that stays for the whole run. Sign it, or refuse and keep your hands clean. He wrote the small print himself.

BUILD YOUR LUCK
Collect 124 charms in ten families, 20 one-use tricks and 16 curses. Pick your Special, work the bench at Pip's stall, and order your charms left to right. Every percentage is on screen.

SIX PEOPLE, ONE HOME
Play Nim, her brother Tao, Auntie Bua, Coach Daeng, Uncle Sok or Pip. Spend your Stash on Nim's home between runs and watch it grow from a cot behind the cart to a penthouse. There are three endings, and the devil has a secret of his own.

EVERY DAY, A NEW RUN
One Daily Run for everyone, with a streak to keep.

Fair play: the game uses virtual coins only. There is no real-money play and there are no prizes. No spin, coin or odds is sold or given for an ad. Purchases are ad removal, one starter pack and optional looks; an optional ad gives a free reroll or doubles the Stash of a run. For adults.
